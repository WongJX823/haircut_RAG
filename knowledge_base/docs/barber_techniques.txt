=== COMMON BARBERING TECHNIQUES ===

FADE TYPES
A fade is a gradual transition from very short hair at the bottom to longer hair at the top. Fades are achieved with clippers using multiple guard sizes.

Skin Fade (Bald Fade): Hair is blended all the way down to the skin. Maximum contrast. Best for oval and round faces needing strong structure. Very trendy and modern.
High Fade: The fade starts high on the head, near or above the temples. Creates maximum contrast between short sides and long top. Best for round faces needing height illusion. Bold and dramatic.
Mid Fade: The fade starts at the temples, the midpoint of the head. A balanced look. Versatile — suits most face shapes. Less extreme than a high fade.
Low Fade: The fade starts just above the ear, keeping more hair on the sides. Subtle and classic. Suits square and heart face shapes where soft contrast is preferred.
Drop Fade: A variation where the fade drops behind the ear, following the head shape. Creates a curved line. Popular with textured and curly hair styles.
Taper: Not a true fade but a gradual shortening without going to skin. More conservative. Suits professional environments. Works on all face shapes.
Temple Fade: Only the temples are faded, leaving the rest of the hair at a uniform length. A subtle, neat look.

SCISSOR TECHNIQUES
Point Cutting: Scissors are held vertically and cut into the ends of the hair to create texture and remove bulk while maintaining length. Used for textured and layered styles.
Slide Cutting: Scissors slide along the hair shaft while cutting to create soft, tapered ends. Used for longer styles and to blend layers.
Blunt Cutting: Scissors are held horizontally and cut across the hair to create a precise, clean line. Used for bob cuts and precise fringes.
Thinning Shears: Special scissors with teeth on one blade that remove weight from thick hair without significantly reducing length. Used on thick and coily hair to control bulk.

RAZOR TECHNIQUES
Razor Fade: Using a straight razor or safety razor to blend fades to the skin for an extra clean finish.
Razor Texturising: Using a razor to add texture to hair ends. Creates very soft, undone finishes popular in shag cuts and wolf cuts.
Razor Line (Lineup): The sharp straight line cut at the hairline on the forehead and temples using a razor or trimmer. Defines the shape of the haircut and gives a very clean finish.

=== HAIR PRODUCTS EXPLAINED ===

POMADE
Provides shine and medium to high hold. Water-based pomades wash out easily. Oil-based pomades last longer but require shampooing to remove. Best for slick back styles, side parts, and pompadours. Suits straight and wavy hair.

CLAY
Provides a matte or low-shine finish with medium hold. Makes hair look thicker and adds texture. Best for textured crops, quiffs, and messy styles. Suits straight, wavy, and fine hair.

WAX
Similar to clay but with more shine. Provides medium hold. Good for defined styles that need to be restyled during the day. Suits straight and wavy hair.

PUTTY
A flexible product between clay and wax. Provides texture and a natural finish with medium hold. Versatile and works on most hair types and styles.

HAIR GEL
High hold with high shine or clear drying finish. Best for slicked styles or keeping curls in place. Can flake if over-applied. Suits curly hair for hold or straight hair for very structured styles.

MOUSSE
A foam product applied to wet hair before styling. Adds volume and hold without weight. Best applied at the roots and distributed through the length. Excellent for wavy and curly hair. Also used for straight hair that needs volume when blow drying.

SEA SALT SPRAY
Adds texture and wave enhancement without heavy hold. Applied to damp or dry hair for a beachy, undone look. Best for wavy and naturally textured hair. Over-use can cause dryness — follow with a light conditioner.

CURL CREAM
A moisturising styling product that defines curl patterns. Applied to wet or damp hair. Works best on curly and coily hair types. Does not provide strong hold but enhances natural curl definition.

LEAVE-IN CONDITIONER
Applied to wet hair after washing and not rinsed out. Provides moisture, reduces frizz, and prepares hair for styling. Essential for curly and coily hair. Beneficial for dry or damaged hair of any type.

HEAT PROTECTANT
Applied before any heat styling tool (blow dryer, flat iron, curling iron). Forms a barrier on the hair shaft to prevent heat damage. Should always be used before applying heat above 150 degrees Celsius. Available in spray, cream, and serum forms.

=== HOW TO COMMUNICATE WITH YOUR BARBER OR STYLIST ===

Describing length: Use specific measurements rather than vague terms. Say "one inch off the top" rather than "a little off". Or specify the clipper guard number for faded sections (e.g. "a 2 on the sides").

Describing fade height: Point to the exact starting point of the fade on your own head rather than using terms like "high" or "mid" which barbers interpret differently.

Bringing reference photos: The most effective way to communicate a desired style. Bring 2-3 photos showing the front, side, and back if possible. Tell the barber which specific elements you like — the fringe, the fade height, the texture on top.

Describing texture preferences: Use terms like "messy and textured", "clean and polished", "soft and natural". Avoid vague terms like "nice" or "neat" which mean different things to different people.

Asking about maintenance: Always ask how often you will need to come back to maintain the cut and what products are recommended at home.

=== GROOMING ROUTINES ===

DAILY ROUTINE FOR SHORT HAIR (MALE)
Apply a small amount of clay or pomade to dry or slightly damp hair. Work through with fingertips. Style into desired shape. Takes 1-2 minutes.

DAILY ROUTINE FOR MEDIUM/LONG HAIR (MALE)
Apply lightweight oil or serum to damp hair after showering. Blow dry with a brush for shape. Finish with pomade or wax if needed for hold. Takes 5-10 minutes.

DAILY ROUTINE FOR STRAIGHT HAIR (FEMALE)
Wash every 2-3 days. Apply heat protectant before blow drying. Blow dry with a round brush for volume or use a paddle brush for smoothness. Finish with a light serum for shine.

DAILY ROUTINE FOR WAVY HAIR (FEMALE)
Wash every 2-3 days. Apply sea salt spray or curl cream to damp hair. Scrunch gently and diffuse or air dry. Avoid touching until fully dry to prevent frizz.

DAILY ROUTINE FOR CURLY HAIR (FEMALE)
Wash 1-3 times per week. Apply leave-in conditioner to soaking wet hair. Add curl cream and scrunch upward. Diffuse on low heat or air dry. Refresh between washes with a water spritz and light cream.

DAILY ROUTINE FOR COILY HAIR (FEMALE)
Wash once per week. Apply LOC method (leave-in, oil, cream) on wash day. Protective styles (braids, twists) reduce daily manipulation. Refresh with a light oil or water spritz as needed.
