ROUND FACE SHAPE OVERVIEW
A round face has similar width and length with soft, curved edges and no strong angles. The cheeks are the widest point and the chin is rounded rather than pointed. The goal when choosing haircuts for a round face is to add height and length to make the face appear more elongated, or to add angles to create definition.

BEST HAIRCUTS FOR ROUND FACE — MALE
High Fade with Volume on Top: Short or shaved sides with significant height on top. The contrast creates a vertical illusion that elongates the face. One of the best cuts for round faces.
Quiff with Height: Similar to an oval quiff but the volume must be pushed higher and more upright to add length to the face visually.
Faux Hawk: A strip of longer hair down the centre of the head with shorter sides. The central height adds vertical emphasis to counteract face width.
Side Part with Comb Over: A clean, structured side part creates an angular line across the forehead that adds definition to a round face. The asymmetry breaks the roundness.
Textured Crop with Fade: Short textured top with faded sides. The texture on top adds visual interest and height without adding width.
Undercut with Side Sweep: Grow hair long enough on top to sweep to one side. The diagonal direction of the hair adds angles to a round face.

BEST HAIRCUTS FOR ROUND FACE — FEMALE
Long Layers: Hair past the shoulders with long layers. Length and layers draw the eye downward, elongating the face.
Side-Swept Bangs: Diagonal fringe that sweeps across the forehead. Creates an asymmetric line that adds angles to a round face. Avoid straight blunt bangs.
Lob with Layers: A long bob with layers. The layers prevent the hair from adding width at cheek level.
High Bun or Topknot: Pulling hair up high adds height and elongates the face. A bun worn high rather than low is much more flattering.
Pixie with Height on Top: A short cut but with height or texture at the crown rather than flat on top.
Curtain Bangs: Parted in the centre and swept to each side. Adds a vertical centre line that lengthens the face.

STYLES TO AVOID FOR ROUND FACE
Bowl cuts: Add width all around the face, emphasising roundness.
Chin-length blunt bobs without layers: The blunt edge at cheek level adds width exactly where you do not want it.
Short crops with no height: Remove volume from the top and make the face look rounder.
Very curly styles with maximum volume at the sides: Increases perceived face width.
Centre-parted straight hair that falls to cheek level: Frames the widest point of the face.
Short styles with volume at the sides (e.g. perm with wide curls): Adds width.

HAIR TYPE TIPS FOR ROUND FACE
Straight hair: Use products to push hair upward. Avoid letting hair hang flat at cheek level.
Wavy hair: Wave texture adds movement. Style waves to fall forward or downward rather than outward.
Curly hair: Pick curls upward to add height rather than width. Avoid letting curls puff out at the sides.
Coily hair: Elongated styles or high afro puffs work well. Taper cuts keep sides neat without adding width.
Fine hair: Volume at the crown is difficult with fine hair. Use volumising mousse and blow dry upside down.
Thick hair: Thick hair can add unwanted width. Request thinning on the sides when visiting a barber.

MAINTENANCE TIPS FOR ROUND FACE HAIRCUTS
High fade: Fades grow out quickly. Visit barber every 2-3 weeks to maintain the contrast.
Side part comb over: Use pomade or wax daily to keep the part defined.
Long layers: Trim every 8-10 weeks. Ask stylist to remove weight from the ends rather than cut length.
Curtain bangs: Grow faster than the rest of hair. Trim or reshape every 4-6 weeks.
