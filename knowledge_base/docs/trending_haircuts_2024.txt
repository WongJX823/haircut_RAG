=== TRENDING MEN'S HAIRCUTS 2024-2025 ===

BUZZ CUT RESURGENCE
The buzz cut has returned as a dominant trend. Variations include the skin fade buzz (where the sides fade to skin), the textured buzz (slightly longer on top with texture), and the high and tight (military-inspired with very short sides). Suits oval and oblong face shapes best. Requires almost no styling product or time. Maintenance: every 2-3 weeks.

TEXTURED CROP
One of the most popular cuts globally. Short back and sides (usually a fade) with a textured, dishevelled top. The fringe sits forward loosely. Works on all hair types but especially good for straight and wavy hair. Product: matte clay or putty worked through damp hair, air dried. Suits round and square face shapes when combined with a high fade for height.

EDGAR CUT (TAKUACHE CUT)
A sharp, blunt horizontal fringe cut straight across with faded sides. Very popular among younger men. The blunt fringe is its defining characteristic. Suits square and oval faces. Best on straight or slightly wavy hair that holds the blunt fringe shape.

CURTAIN BANGS (MALE)
Centre-parted hair that falls loosely to each side, reminiscent of 1970s style. Works on medium length hair. Extremely flattering on oval, heart, and round faces. Easy to style with minimal product. A sea salt spray and quick blow dry while parting down the centre is sufficient.

FLUFFY HAIR / CLOUD HAIR
Voluminous, lifted, soft-looking hair worn in natural texture. Associated with Korean and East Asian beauty trends (K-pop influence). Achieved by blow drying hair upward with a round brush and using a light-hold product. Suits oval and heart face shapes. Works best on straight and fine hair that lifts easily.

WOLF CUT (MALE)
Inspired by 1970s shag cuts. Layered heavily throughout with a short crown and longer, wispy ends. The combination of a mullet structure and shag layers creates a high-volume, textured look. Suits oval and oblong face shapes. Works well on wavy and curly hair types that naturally add volume to the layers.

SKIN FADE VARIATIONS
Skin fades remain extremely popular across all haircut styles. The bald fade (skin-level), high skin fade, mid skin fade, and low skin fade give different amounts of contrast. High skin fades are best for round faces needing height contrast. Low fades suit square and heart faces better, maintaining some hair around the temples.

FRENCH CROP
A short cut with a textured or blunt horizontal fringe, faded or tapered sides, and a textured top. Very low maintenance. Suits square and oval faces. Works on straight, wavy, and fine hair. Product: light wax or nothing at all.

=== TRENDING WOMEN'S HAIRCUTS 2024-2025 ===

WOLF CUT (FEMALE)
The biggest trend of recent years for women. Combines a shag cut and a mullet with heavy layers, a short crown, and long wispy ends. Extremely adaptable — suits oval, round, and heart face shapes. Works especially well on wavy and curly hair that shows off the layers. Fine hair may struggle to hold the volume.

CURTAIN BANGS (FEMALE)
Soft, parted bangs that frame the face by sweeping to each side. Versatile enough to work with any hair length. One of the most universally flattering fringe styles. Suits all face shapes but especially heart and round faces. Grow out gracefully into side-swept bangs.

BIXIE CUT
A hybrid between a bob and a pixie. Longer than a pixie but shorter than a traditional bob. Sits around the jaw or just above. Suits oval and heart face shapes particularly well. Works on all hair textures. Easy to style and maintain.

THE UNDONE BOB
A bob cut worn with deliberately imperfect, undone texture rather than blowout smoothness. Achieved with texturising spray and finger styling rather than a brush. Suits oval and square face shapes. Works on straight and wavy hair.

LAYERED LOBS
The lob (long bob) with heavy internal layers remains one of the most requested salon cuts globally. The layers add movement and reduce weight in thick hair. Suits all face shapes depending on how it is styled. Very adaptable to straight, wavy, and curly hair.

BLUNT BOB COMEBACK
The sharp, blunt bob with no layers is trending again. Best for straight and fine hair that shows the clean line. Suits oval and heart face shapes. The sharpness of the blunt edge requires regular trimming every 6-8 weeks to maintain.

SHAG CUT
A heavily layered cut with a choppy fringe, popularised in the 1970s and returning now. Maximum texture and movement. Suits wavy and curly hair perfectly because the layers show off natural texture. Works on oval, round, and heart face shapes. Avoid on very fine hair that may look stringy with too many layers.

=== HAIRCUT MAINTENANCE GENERAL GUIDE ===

FREQUENCY BY STYLE
Very short cuts (buzz, fade, crop): Every 2-4 weeks
Short to medium cuts (bob, lob, textured): Every 6-8 weeks
Long cuts (layers, curtain bangs): Every 10-12 weeks for the length, bangs every 4 weeks

PRODUCT GUIDE BY STYLE
Matte and textured looks: Use clay, putty, or paste. Apply to damp or dry hair.
Sleek and polished looks: Use pomade, wax, or gel. Apply to damp hair for maximum hold.
Natural and wave-enhanced looks: Use sea salt spray or curl cream. Apply to wet hair and diffuse.
Volume-based looks: Use mousse at the root before blow drying. Blow dry upward with a round brush.
Curly and coily natural styles: Use leave-in conditioner and curl cream on soaking wet hair. Diffuse.

WHEN TO VISIT YOUR BARBER OR STYLIST
Visit when hair starts to lose its shape, not just when it gets long. Most cuts look best for only 2-6 weeks before the shape begins to break down. Regular visits maintain the look and make each visit quicker and less expensive.
