Source: Curated guide — Haircuts for Square and Heart Face Shapes

=== SQUARE FACE SHAPE ===

SQUARE FACE SHAPE OVERVIEW
A square face has a strong, angular jawline that is roughly the same width as the forehead. Cheekbones, forehead, and jaw are all similar in width. The goal is either to soften the angular jaw with texture and movement, or to embrace and complement the strong structure with clean, defined cuts.

BEST HAIRCUTS FOR SQUARE FACE — MALE
Textured Crop: Short to medium length with choppy, textured ends. The softness of the texture reduces the harshness of the square jawline.
Messy Quiff: A quiff styled messily rather than slicked back. The relaxed texture softens the angular face.
Side Swept Fringe: Hair swept diagonally across the forehead. The diagonal line contrasts the horizontal line of the square jaw and adds softness.
Medium Length with Layers: Hair grown to ear or chin length with layers. Layers add movement that softens square angles.
Classic Taper: Short sides that gradually taper without a hard contrast. Softer than a high fade and more flattering on very angular faces.
French Crop: Short textured fringe sitting flat on the forehead. The horizontal fringe softens the strong forehead line of a square face.

BEST HAIRCUTS FOR SQUARE FACE — FEMALE
Soft Waves or Curls: Wavy or curly styling at jaw level softens the angular structure. One of the most flattering choices for square faces.
Long Layers: Length past the jaw with soft layers. The movement and length draw the eye downward and soften the jaw.
Side Part with Loose Styling: A deep side part with loose, relaxed hair. The asymmetry breaks the symmetrical rigidity of the square face.
Wispy Bangs: Soft, thin bangs that brush the forehead lightly. Much more flattering than blunt thick bangs on square faces.
Pixie with Soft Fringe: A short cut but with a soft, textured fringe rather than a blunt one.
Bob with Waves: A bob that ends at the jaw but with wave or curl styling to soften the angular edge.

STYLES TO AVOID FOR SQUARE FACE
Very short sides with a flat top (military style): Emphasises the square shape by adding a horizontal top line to match the jaw.
Blunt straight fringes: Create a strong horizontal line at the forehead, echoing the strong lines already present.
Slicked-back hair with very defined sides: Removes all softness and makes angles more prominent.
Hair pulled tightly back: Exposes the full angular structure with no softening.

HAIR TYPE TIPS FOR SQUARE FACE
Straight hair: Add texture with a matte clay or salt spray. Avoid styles that lie completely flat.
Wavy hair: Natural wave texture is ideal for square faces. Let waves fall naturally to soften the jaw.
Curly hair: Defined curls at jaw level are highly flattering. Avoid very tight close-cropped styles that emphasise the jawline.
Fine hair: Shorter cuts with texture work well. Avoid very long thin straight hair that hangs stiffly.

MAINTENANCE TIPS FOR SQUARE FACE
Textured crop: Use matte clay or putty daily. Scrunch product into damp hair and air dry for best texture.
Soft waves: Use a diffuser on curly or wavy hair to enhance natural movement. Avoid flat ironing.
Medium length with layers: Trim every 8 weeks to maintain the shape of layers. Layers grow out quickly.

=== HEART FACE SHAPE ===

HEART FACE SHAPE OVERVIEW
A heart-shaped face is widest at the forehead and narrows to a pointed or small chin. The goal is to balance the wider forehead with styles that add width lower down near the chin, or that minimise the forehead width.

BEST HAIRCUTS FOR HEART FACE — MALE
Side Part: A classic side part draws attention away from a wide forehead. The asymmetry reduces the visual width at the top.
Medium Length Textured Styles: Hair grown to medium length with texture. Allows volume to sit lower on the face near the jaw, balancing the forehead.
Textured Fringe: A fringe that partially covers the forehead reduces its visual prominence.
Longer Styles with Volume at Jaw: Hair grown long enough for volume to appear at jaw level. Balances the wide forehead by adding perceived width lower.
Low Fade: A fade that keeps more hair on the sides lower down. Contrasts less with the wide forehead than a high fade would.
Curtain Bangs (male): Centre-parted hair that falls to each side. Partially covers the forehead while adding width at cheekbone level.

BEST HAIRCUTS FOR HEART FACE — FEMALE
Long Hair with Volume at Ends: Hair grown past the shoulder with curls or waves at the ends adds width near the chin level to balance the forehead.
Side Swept Bangs: A diagonal fringe covering part of the forehead reduces its width visually.
Lob with Waves: The length and waves add width at jaw and chin level. A very flattering combination for heart faces.
Centre Part with Long Hair: Creates a vertical central line that draws the eye downward rather than across the wide forehead.
Chin-Length Bob: Adds width at the chin level to balance the narrow lower face.
Curtain Bangs: Soft bangs parted in the middle and swept to the sides. Partially covers the forehead without hiding the face.

STYLES TO AVOID FOR HEART FACE
High volume on top: Adds even more visual width to an already wide forehead.
Very short crops with no fringe: Exposes the full forehead width with nothing to soften it.
Styles with maximum volume at the temples: The widest point is already the forehead — adding volume there increases imbalance.
Very sleek tight ponytails or updos: Pull all hair back and expose the wide forehead and narrow chin contrast.

HAIR TYPE TIPS FOR HEART FACE
Straight hair: Use a deep side part and let hair fall to one side to cover part of the forehead.
Wavy hair: Let waves develop from the cheekbones downward. Avoid volume concentrated at the crown.
Curly hair: Pull curls into styles that frame the lower face. Pineapple updos can expose the forehead too much.
Coily hair: Tapered styles with more volume at the sides of the lower face are flattering.
Fine hair: Volume is harder to achieve at jaw level. Use volumising products concentrated at the ends.
Thick hair: Thick hair naturally adds the volume needed at the lower face. Keep the top flatter.

MAINTENANCE TIPS FOR HEART FACE
Side swept bangs: Trim every 3-4 weeks as bangs grow fast and quickly lose their shape.
Lob with waves: Use a salt spray or curl cream on damp hair and diffuse. Trim every 6-8 weeks.
Medium length textured male styles: Reshape every 5-6 weeks. Use medium hold product daily.
