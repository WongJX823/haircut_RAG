Source: Curated Haircut Style Guide

=== OVAL FACE SHAPE ===
Oval face shape is considered the most versatile face shape for haircuts. Almost all hairstyles suit oval faces.
Best styles: Crew cut, quiff, slick back, undercut, pompadour, textured fringe.
Avoid: Extremely long hair on top that elongates the face further.
Hair type compatibility: Works with straight, wavy, curly, and coily hair.

=== ROUND FACE SHAPE ===
Round faces have similar width and length with soft angles. The goal is to add height and length.
Best styles: High fade with volume on top, quiff, faux hawk, side part, comb over with height.
Avoid: Bowl cuts, short crops with no height, styles that add width to the sides.
For curly hair: Pick out curls upward to add height rather than width.
For straight hair: Use pomade to push hair upward and create vertical lines.

=== SQUARE FACE SHAPE ===
Square faces have a strong jawline and angular features. Soften the jaw or complement the structure.
Best styles: Textured crop, messy quiff, side swept fringe, soft layers that frame the face.
Avoid: Very short sides with flat tops (emphasises squareness), blunt straight fringes.
For wavy hair: Let natural waves add softness to angular features.
For straight hair: Add texture with styling products to avoid harsh lines.

=== HEART FACE SHAPE ===
Heart faces are wider at the forehead and narrow at the chin. Balance the forehead and chin width.
Best styles: Side parts, styles with volume near the chin, textured medium length cuts.
Avoid: High volume on top (makes forehead appear wider), very short sides.
For curly hair: Allow curls to frame the face from mid-length down.

=== STRAIGHT HAIR ===
Straight hair lies flat and can look limp without product. Works best with structured cuts.
Best styles: Undercut, slick back, side part, crew cut.
Tips: Use volumising mousse at roots. Blow dry with a round brush for lift.
Avoid: Very long unstyled hair that hangs flat.

=== WAVY HAIR ===
Wavy hair has natural texture and movement. One of the easiest hair types to style.
Best styles: Textured crop, quiff, modern fringe, medium length styles.
Tips: Use sea salt spray to enhance natural waves. Diffuse when drying.
Avoid: Styles requiring perfectly straight lines unless using straightener.

=== CURLY HAIR ===
Curly hair shrinks when dry. Always consider the expanded size when choosing length.
Best styles: Curly top with fade, twist out, defined curl styles, medium afro.
Tips: Use curl defining cream. Avoid brushing dry — use wide tooth comb on wet hair.
Avoid: Layered cuts that can make curls triangle-shaped and puffy at the bottom.

=== COILY HAIR ===
Coily hair (Type 4) has tight coils and maximum shrinkage. Moisture retention is key.
Best styles: Afro, tapered cut, loc styles, twist out, wash and go.
Tips: Deep condition weekly. Use LOC method (Liquid, Oil, Cream) for moisture.
Avoid: Frequent heat styling which causes breakage in coily hair.

=== FINE HAIR TEXTURE ===
Fine hair has thin individual strands. Creates volume carefully.
Best styles: Short to medium cuts, blunt cuts to create appearance of thickness.
Avoid: Very long heavy styles that weigh fine hair down.
Product tip: Lightweight volumising mousse, avoid heavy waxes or pomades.

=== MEDIUM HAIR TEXTURE ===
Medium texture is the most versatile. Holds styles well and has good body.
Compatible with most haircut styles. Focus on face shape as the primary factor.

=== THICK HAIR TEXTURE ===
Thick hair has dense, heavy strands. Can be difficult to manage without the right cut.
Best styles: Cuts with thinning or layering to reduce bulk.
Avoid: Very blunt cuts that emphasise the bulk.
Product tip: Use smoothing serum or cream to control frizz and weight.
