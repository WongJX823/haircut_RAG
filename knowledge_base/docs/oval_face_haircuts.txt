OVAL FACE SHAPE OVERVIEW
Oval is considered the most balanced and versatile face shape. The forehead is slightly wider than the chin, and the face length is about one and a half times the width. Cheekbones are the widest point. Almost every haircut works well for oval faces because the proportions are already balanced.

BEST HAIRCUTS FOR OVAL FACE — MALE
Crew Cut: A timeless short back and sides with slightly longer hair on top. Works perfectly on oval faces because it does not add unnecessary width or height. Easy to maintain and suits all hair textures.
Quiff: Volume is pushed upward and slightly back at the front. The vertical height complements the oval shape without distorting it. Works best with straight or wavy hair.
Undercut: Shaved or very short sides with longer hair on top styled in any direction. Extremely versatile for oval faces. The contrast between sides and top adds definition.
Slick Back: Hair is grown medium length and combed straight back using pomade or gel. Oval faces carry this look effortlessly because no extra volume is needed to balance proportions.
Textured Fringe: A modern cut where the fringe falls loosely over the forehead with texture. Suits oval faces of all hair types.
Pompadour: Dramatic volume swept upward and back. Oval faces are one of the few shapes that can carry the height of a pompadour without looking unbalanced.
Caesar Cut: Short fringe cut horizontally across the forehead. Suits oval faces because the face shape needs no correction.
Buzz Cut: A very short uniform cut all over. Oval faces suit buzz cuts better than any other face shape because the balanced proportions still look good with minimal hair.

BEST HAIRCUTS FOR OVAL FACE — FEMALE
Lob (Long Bob): A bob cut that falls to the collarbone. Flattering on oval faces because it frames without hiding the natural balance.
Layers: Long hair with layers adds movement and dimension without disrupting the oval balance.
Bangs: Almost any bang style works on oval faces — blunt, side-swept, curtain, or wispy.
Pixie Cut: Short cropped cut. Oval faces carry pixie cuts very well because the facial structure remains visible and attractive.
Beach Waves: Medium to long length with loose wave styling. One of the most flattering looks for oval faces.
Bob: Classic chin-length bob. Clean and balanced on oval faces.

STYLES TO AVOID FOR OVAL FACE
Very long flat hair with no layers or volume can make an oval face appear too elongated.
Extremely tall styles with high volume at the crown can push the face shape into an oblong appearance.

HAIR TYPE COMPATIBILITY FOR OVAL FACE
Straight hair: Most styles work. Use volumising products for styles requiring body.
Wavy hair: Natural texture adds movement that flatters the oval shape naturally.
Curly hair: Let curls fall naturally or pick them upward — both work well.
Coily hair: Afro styles and tapered cuts suit oval faces excellently.
Fine hair: Short to medium cuts are easiest to manage and still look great.
Thick hair: May need thinning or layering to reduce bulk, but all styles are achievable.

MAINTENANCE TIPS FOR OVAL FACE HAIRCUTS
Crew cut and buzz cut: Trim every 3-4 weeks to maintain shape.
Quiff and pompadour: Use medium-hold pomade or clay. Blow dry with a round brush for volume.
Undercut: Sides need trimming every 3-4 weeks. Top can grow longer between visits.
Lob and bob: Trim every 6-8 weeks to prevent split ends and maintain shape.
