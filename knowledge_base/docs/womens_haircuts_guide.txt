=== WOMEN'S HAIRCUTS COMPREHENSIVE GUIDE ===

SHORT HAIRCUTS FOR WOMEN

Pixie Cut
A short cropped cut typically with short sides and a slightly longer top. Can be styled smooth, textured, or with a fringe. Suits oval and heart face shapes best. On a round face, a pixie with height on top works best. On a square face, choose a pixie with a soft fringe to avoid harsh lines. Requires regular trimming every 4-6 weeks to maintain the shape. Works on straight, wavy, and fine hair. Thick hair may require thinning.

Pixie Bob (Pob)
A longer version of the pixie that sits between chin and ear length. More versatile than a pixie. Suits all face shapes. The length around the face can be customised to flatter the face shape.

Buzz Cut (Female)
A bold and increasingly popular choice. Removes almost all hair length. Suits oval, diamond, and heart face shapes best where the facial bone structure is strong and defined. Requires confidence and commitment. Maintenance is minimal — simply keep it even.

BOB HAIRCUTS FOR WOMEN

Classic Chin-Length Bob
Falls at or just below the chin. The most universally known bob. Best for oval, heart, and oblong face shapes. Can be worn with or without bangs. Blunt bobs suit straight hair. Textured bobs suit wavy and curly hair.

A-Line Bob (Stacked Bob)
Shorter at the back and longer at the front, creating an angled line. The angle adds definition to the chin area. Suits oval and round face shapes. The front length draws attention to the jaw. Works well on straight and slightly wavy hair.

Inverted Bob
A bob with a steeply angled line from back to front. Creates a dramatic silhouette. Suits oval and heart face shapes. The length in front frames the face while the short back keeps it tidy.

Blunt Bob
No layers, cut in a single horizontal line. Very clean and modern. Best on straight or fine hair that shows the precise line. Suits oval and heart face shapes. Square and round faces may find the blunt line too strong. Requires trimming every 5-7 weeks to maintain precision.

Textured Bob
A bob with choppy layers or undone texture. More forgiving than a blunt bob and suits more face shapes including round and square because the texture softens lines. Works on wavy and curly hair naturally.

LOB (LONG BOB) FOR WOMEN
Falls between the chin and the collarbone. The most popular haircut length globally. Suits all face shapes depending on styling. Layered lobs suit round faces. Blunt lobs suit oval and heart faces. Wavy lobs suit square faces. The lob is extremely versatile.

MEDIUM LENGTH HAIRCUTS FOR WOMEN

Shag Cut
Heavy layers throughout, a short crown, and choppy fringe. Maximum texture and volume. Originated in the 1970s. Suits oval, round, and heart face shapes. Best on wavy and curly hair. Fine hair may look wispy with too many layers.

Rachel Cut / 90s Layers
Long layers beginning at the chin level. Named after the iconic Friends haircut. Suits oval and heart face shapes. Very flattering with a side part. Works on straight and wavy hair.

Layered Mid-Length Cut
Hair falling to the shoulder or just below with multiple internal layers. The layers add movement and reduce weight. Suits all face shapes. One of the most requested cuts globally because of its versatility.

LONG HAIRCUTS FOR WOMEN

Long Layers
The most popular long hairstyle. Long layers beginning at or below the shoulders. Adds movement without reducing length significantly. Suits all face shapes. Works on all hair types. Layering is especially important for thick and curly hair to remove excess weight.

Mermaid Layers
Extremely long hair with layers only beginning from mid-length downward. The long length flatters oval and oblong face shapes. Requires deep conditioning to maintain health at the ends.

BANGS / FRINGE FOR WOMEN

Blunt Bangs (Full Fringe)
Straight across the forehead above the eyebrows. Can shorten a long face. Suits oval and oblong face shapes best. On a round face they add width. On a heart face they draw attention to the wide forehead. Require trimming every 3-4 weeks.

Side-Swept Bangs
Diagonal bangs that sweep to one side across the forehead. One of the most universally flattering bang styles. Works on all face shapes. On round and heart faces, the diagonal line adds angles and balance. Easier to grow out than blunt bangs.

Curtain Bangs
Parted in the centre and swept to each side. Soft and romantic. Suits oval, round, and heart face shapes. Grow out very gracefully into side-swept bangs. Work on straight, wavy, and curly hair.

Micro Bangs
Very short bangs sitting above the eyebrows. A bold statement. Suits oval face shapes best where they sit above strong cheekbones. Require very regular trimming.

Wispy Bangs
Thin, light bangs with gaps rather than a full solid fringe. Very soft and easy to wear. Suits almost all face shapes because the openness prevents them from overpowering the face.

=== COLOUR AND CUT COMBINATIONS ===

Balayage with Long Layers: The graduated colour technique is best shown through long layers because the colour transitions are visible in the movement. Suits all face shapes with long hair.
Highlights with Bob: Short face-framing highlights draw attention to the jawline. Best on oval and heart face shapes.
Face-Framing Highlights: Lighter pieces framing the face brighten the skin tone and draw attention to facial features. Works on all cut lengths and face shapes.

=== HAIR CARE FOR WOMEN ===

Preventing Split Ends: Trim every 8-12 weeks even when growing hair. Split ends cause breakage that shortens hair over time.
Heat Styling: Always use a heat protectant spray before flat ironing or curling. Limit heat to 180 degrees Celsius for fine hair and 200 degrees for thick hair.
Overnight Care: Sleep on a silk or satin pillowcase to reduce friction and frizz, especially for curly and coily hair.
Scalp Health: A healthy scalp produces healthy hair. Use a scalp scrub monthly to remove buildup. Avoid applying heavy products directly to the scalp.
